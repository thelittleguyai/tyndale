# Document-request guidance (V1-Lite)

> mode: V1-Lite.

**Purpose.** When coverage info is incomplete, ask the user for the specific missing
document — and tell them exactly where to find it (P1).

**Which document, and why.**
- **Insurance card (photo)** — fastest source of payer, member ID, plan type, and often the
  deductible/copay basics. *Where:* physical card or the insurer's app/portal "ID card."
- **Summary of Benefits and Coverage (SBC)** — the authoritative coverage terms (deductible,
  coinsurance, OOP max, network tiers). *Where:* member portal under "plan documents" /
  "benefits," or from HR for employer plans.
- **EOB** — the insurer's CLAIM about what was covered and owed. *Where:* member portal under
  "claims." Request a resend if missing.

**Prioritization.** Get the **SBC and the insurance card first** — they carry the COVERAGE
TERMS that power the independent audit. Get the **EOB** as the insurer's CLAIM to be audited
(NOT as the source of the right answer).

**Tone.** Ask for the one document that unlocks the most (P1), with the exact place to find
it. If the user can't get it, switch to `helping_the_user_find_coverage_info.md` — never
"go figure it out."
