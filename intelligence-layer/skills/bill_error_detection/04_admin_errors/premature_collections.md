# Premature collections

**What this is.** A bill is sent to collections (or an extraordinary collection action is
taken) before required notice, before the EOB posted, or — for a nonprofit hospital —
before reasonable efforts to determine financial-assistance eligibility.

**Detection signals.**
- Collections activity while the claim/EOB is still pending.
- A nonprofit hospital pursuing collections without offering its Financial Assistance
  Policy (FAP).
- No itemized bill or notice provided before the collections referral.

**Citation language.** Tier B for nonprofit hospitals: "A tax-exempt hospital may not
undertake extraordinary collection actions before making reasonable efforts to determine
financial-assistance eligibility [IRS §501(r)(6), 26 U.S.C. §501(r)(6), src_TBD]."

**Severity.** High (credit impact; legal protections may be violated).

**Common defenses.** Provider claims notice was given. Response: require proof of notice and
of FAP screening (for nonprofits).

**Required evidence.** The collections notice, the bill/EOB timeline, and the hospital's FAP.

**Recommended remediation (Tier C).** Demand the account be recalled pending adjudication /
FAP screening; sequence via `negotiation_strategy` (`collections_dispute`, `charity_care`).
